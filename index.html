Research

Areas of focus

Exploring how quantum phenomena manifest in atomically thin crystals and their heterostructures.

01

2D Materials & Heterostructures

Investigating the electronic and optical properties of transition metal dichalcogenides and their van der Waals heterostructures.

02

Optical Spectroscopy

Using Raman, photoluminescence, and reflectance spectroscopy to probe phonon dynamics, excitons, and many-body interactions in layered materials.

03

Device Physics

Fabricating and characterizing devices based on 2D materials to explore quantum transport, optoelectronic response, and emergent phenomena.
