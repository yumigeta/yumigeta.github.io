Research

Areas of focus

From atomically precise synthesis to functional nanoelectronic devices — bridging fundamental materials science and scalable fabrication.

01

Graphene Nanoribbons & Nanoelectronics

Atomically precise GNR synthesis, scalable etch-free transfer, and transistor fabrication for high-performance computing and radiation sensing.

02

Charge Density Waves

Engineering CDW phases in rare-earth tritellurides through alloying, pressure, and temperature — probing stability, phonon dynamics, and magnetic coupling.

03

2D Materials & Heterostructures

van der Waals heterostructures, moiré superlattices, exciton physics, and polaritonics in transition metal dichalcogenides.
